OFFICIAL LIST REQUIREMENTS

YBCSPCL Rules.

These rules establish the requirements for challenges submitted to the YBC Spam Challenge List. Read them carefully before submitting a challenge or completion.

Official Rules

All challenges and records must follow these requirements.

1

The 30 CPS Limit

Challenges must be possible within the official 30 clicks per second (CPS) limit. A challenge that requires a clicking rate above 30 CPS does not qualify for the list.

2

Publicly Available Levels

Challenges must be publicly accessible in Geometry Dash so that other players can find and attempt them. Provide the level name and enough information for the team to locate it.

3

Completion Evidence

Every submitted completion must include sufficient evidence for the list staff to verify that the challenge was completed. Evidence may be rejected if it is unclear or cannot be properly checked.

4

Legitimate Completions

Completions must be achieved legitimately. Evidence must not be fabricated or edited to misrepresent a completion. Staff may request additional proof when necessary.

5

Fair Challenge Design

Submitted challenges must be genuine spam challenges and should not rely on bugs, exploits or unintended behaviour to meet the list's requirements.

6

Staff Decisions

List staff may reject submissions that do not meet the requirements or lack sufficient evidence. Challenges may be reviewed again if new information becomes available.

Important

Submitting a challenge does not guarantee acceptance. Staff will review whether it meets the requirements before adding it to the official rankings.
