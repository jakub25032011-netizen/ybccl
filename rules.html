Official Rules

All levels submitted to the YBCCL must follow these rules. Levels that fail to meet the requirements may be rejected or removed from the list.

01

Publicly Available Levels

All submitted levels must be publicly accessible through your Geometry Dash profile and available on Geometry Dash servers.

02

240 FPS Compatibility

All levels must be possible to complete at 240 FPS. Levels that require a higher frame rate to be completed are not eligible for the YBCCL.

03

13 CPS Limit

Levels must not contain spam sections that cannot reasonably be completed at 13 clicks per second (CPS).

04

Originality

Do not copy large sections of other levels or copy entire rated levels into your own level. Submissions must contain original work.

05

Completion Evidence

Every submitted level must have evidence of a successful completion. The evidence must allow the completion to be reasonably verified.
